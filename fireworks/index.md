# Fireworks Technical References

Technical documentation for fireworks modeling in games and 3D applications. Scientific approach with engineering principles, physics, and industry standards.

### 1. Mortar Tubes - Physics & Engineering
Comprehensive technical guide to fireworks mortar tubes covering material science, pressure dynamics, ballistics, and regulatory standards.

- **Reference:** [[mortar-tubes|Mortar Tubes - Technical Reference]]

**Topics:**
- Pressure dynamics & hoop stress calculations
- DR (Dimension Ratio) system: DR11, DR17, DR21
- Material science: HDPE, fiberglass/FRE, cardboard, steel
- Ballistics & trajectory physics
- Launch velocity & apex height formulas
- Regional standards: NFPA (US), EN (EU), GB (China)
- Inspection & maintenance protocols

### 2. Rack Systems - Configuration & Safety
Engineering guide to rack configurations, structural requirements, safety standards, and operational procedures.

- **Reference:** [[racks|Rack Systems - Technical Reference]]

**Topics:**

- NFPA spacing requirements & safety distances
- Fan angle geometry (V-fan, W-fan)
- Standard configurations: consumer modular, professional bar, wood crate
- Setup procedures & firing sequences
- Material selection by load class
- Storage & maintenance
- Game development guidelines

### 3. Effects Types
Visual guide to common firework shell effects for game VFX and animation.

- **Reference:** [[effects-types|Fireworks Effects Types Reference]]

**Topics:** Spherical breaks (peony, chrysanthemum, dahlia), falling effects (willow, palm, waterfall), specialty effects (brocade, kamuro, spider, crossette), visual modifiers (pistil, strobe, glitter), break patterns, game implementation notes

### 4. Chemistry & Colors
Chemical compounds and color production in fireworks for accurate game rendering.

- **Reference:** [[chemistry-colors|Fireworks Chemistry & Colors]]

**Topics:** Color production mechanism, primary color compounds (red/strontium, green/barium, blue/copper, etc.), metallic effects, color mixing, brightness scale, distance visibility, game color palette, HDR/bloom values
