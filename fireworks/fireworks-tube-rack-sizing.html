📐 Mortar Tube Specifications

Shell-to-Tube Size Matching

Critical Rule: Shell diameter phải nhỏ hơn tube inner diameter để có clearance cho lift charge pressure.

Shell Size → Tube ID Requirement

Shell OD + Clearance = Minimum Tube ID

Typical Clearance: 0.05-0.1 inches (1.3-2.5 mm) radial

Tube Outer Diameter Calculations

Tube OD = Tube ID + (Wall Thickness × 2)

Examples:
- 1.75" shell tube: 1.8" ID + (0.1" × 2) = 2.0" OD
- 3.0" shell tube: 3.0" ID + (0.15" × 2) = 3.3" OD
- 6.0" shell tube: 6.0" ID + (0.25" × 2) = 6.5" OD

Tube Length Requirements

Formula:

Minimum Tube Length ≥ Shell Height + (Shell Diameter × 0.5) + 2"

Shell Height ≈ Shell Diameter × 1.0-1.2
(sphere to cylinder ratio)

🔧 Mortar Rack Specifications

Rack Design Principles

Key Factors:
1. Tube spacing (center-to-center)
2. Rack stability (width/height ratio)
3. Anchoring method
4. Safety orientation

Tube Spacing Requirements

Formula:

Center-to-Center Spacing = Tube OD + Minimum Gap

Minimum Gap = 0.5-1.0 inches (1.3-2.5 cm)

Wooden Rack Construction

Material Specifications

Base Plate:
  - Material: 3/4" (19 mm) plywood
  - Grade: Exterior or marine plywood
  - Treatment: Weather-resistant finish optional

Side Supports:
  - Material: 3/4" (19 mm) plywood or 1x4 pine boards
  - Height: Tube length + 1-2" (2.5-5 cm)
  - Thickness: Minimum 3/4" (19 mm)

Cross Braces:
  - Material: 1x3 or 1x4 boards (19 x 89 mm)
  - Spacing: 10-12" (25.4-30.5 cm) apart

Rack Dimensions by Tube Count

6-Tube Rack (1.75” shells):

Base Plate: 24" × 6" × 3/4" (61 × 15.2 × 1.9 cm)
Side Supports: 12" × 6" × 3/4" (30.5 × 15.2 × 1.9 cm) [2 pieces]
Tube Spacing: 3" (7.6 cm) center-to-center
Total Width: 18" (45.7 cm) [3" × 6 tubes]
Weight: ~5-7 lbs (2.3-3.2 kg) empty

10-Tube Rack (1.75” shells):

Base Plate: 36" × 6" × 3/4" (91.4 × 15.2 × 1.9 cm)
Side Supports: 12" × 6" × 3/4" (30.5 × 15.2 × 1.9 cm) [2 pieces]
Tube Spacing: 3" (7.6 cm) center-to-center
Total Width: 30" (76.2 cm)
Weight: ~8-10 lbs (3.6-4.5 kg) empty

6-Tube Rack (3” shells):

Base Plate: 30" × 8" × 3/4" (76.2 × 20.3 × 1.9 cm)
Side Supports: 24" × 8" × 3/4" (61 × 20.3 × 1.9 cm) [2 pieces]
Tube Spacing: 5" (12.7 cm) center-to-center
Total Width: 30" (76.2 cm)
Weight: ~12-15 lbs (5.4-6.8 kg) empty

Fastener Specifications

Wood Screws:
  - Size: #8 × 1.5-2" (3.8-5 cm) for 3/4" plywood
  - Type: Exterior-grade, galvanized or stainless
  - Placement: 6" (15.2 cm) spacing along joints
  - Head: Countersunk flat head

⚠️ Critical: Never drive fasteners through tube mounting area

Metal Rack Construction

Skylighter PL3175 Style (1.75” Tubes)

Frame Material: Steel square tubing
  - Size: 1" × 1" × 1/16" (2.54 × 2.54 × 0.16 cm)
  - Weight: 16 gauge steel
  
Tube Holders: Metal clips
  - Material: 18 gauge steel
  - Diameter: 2.1" (5.3 cm) internal
  - Mounting: Welded or bolted to frame

Dimensions (6-tube):
  - Width: 18" (45.7 cm)
  - Height: 12" (30.5 cm)
  - Depth (with feet): 24" (61 cm)
  - Weight: 8-10 lbs (3.6-4.5 kg) empty

Feet:
  - Length: 12" (30.5 cm) each
  - Width: 2" (5.1 cm)
  - Swing-out design
  - Spike holes: 3/8" (9.5 mm) diameter

Heavy-Duty Professional Rack (3”-6” Tubes)

Frame Material: Steel square tubing
  - Size: 1.5" × 1.5" × 1/8" (3.8 × 3.8 × 0.32 cm)
  - Weight: 11 gauge steel

Base Plate: Steel sheet
  - Thickness: 1/4" (6.4 mm)
  - Reinforcement ribs every 12" (30.5 cm)

Tube Holders: Steel collars
  - Thickness: 1/8" (3.2 mm)
  - U-bolt or clamp mounting
  - Rubber padding optional

Weight: 30-50 lbs (13.6-22.7 kg) per 6-tube rack

Rack Stability & Anchoring

Stability Ratio

Width/Height Ratio for Free-Standing Racks:

Minimum Ratio = 2:1 (base width : rack height)
Recommended Ratio = 3:1 for high-wind areas

Example (1.75" tubes, 12" height):
  Minimum base width: 24" (61 cm)
  Recommended: 36" (91.4 cm)

Anchoring Methods

Method 1: Buried Tubes

Burial Depth: 2/3 of tube length
  - 10" tube → bury 6.67" (16.9 cm)
  - 12" tube → bury 8" (20.3 cm)
  - 18" tube → bury 12" (30.5 cm)
  - 24" tube → bury 16" (40.6 cm)

Hole Diameter: Tube OD + 0.5" (1.3 cm)
Backfill: Compacted soil or sand

Method 2: Rebar Pins

Screw Eyes:
  - Size: 1/4" (6.4 mm) diameter
  - Length: 2" (5.1 cm) threaded into wood
  - Position: Each corner of rack

Rebar Pins:
  - Diameter: 3/8" (9.5 mm) or 1/2" (12.7 mm)
  - Length: 18-24" (45.7-61 cm)
  - Ground penetration: 12-16" (30.5-40.6 cm)
  
Spacing: One pin between two racks (shared)

Method 3: Swing-Out Feet with Stakes

Foot Specifications:
  - Length: 12-18" (30.5-45.7 cm)
  - Width: 2-3" (5.1-7.6 cm)
  - Material: Wood or steel

Spike Holes:
  - Diameter: 3/8" (9.5 mm)
  - Position: 2" (5.1 cm) from each end

Stakes/Spikes:
  - Length: 10-12" (25.4-30.5 cm)
  - Diameter: 3/8" (9.5 mm)
  - Material: Steel tent stakes or rebar

Method 4: Sandbag Weighting

For Temporary Setups:

Weight Required (per rack):
  - 1.75" shells: 40-50 lbs (18-23 kg)
  - 3" shells: 80-100 lbs (36-45 kg)
  - 6" shells: 150-200 lbs (68-91 kg)

Placement: Evenly distributed on base
Material: Sandbags, weight plates, or water jugs

Rack Orientation & Safety

Perpendicular Orientation (Standard)

Setup Rule: Rack ends MUST be perpendicular to crowd

Why: If rack falls, tubes fire away from crowd, not toward

Visual:
  
  Side View:
  
  Crowd → ⚠️  [===========]  ← Rack (perpendicular)
                │││││││
                Tubes
  
  If rack falls forward/backward:
  Shells fire left/right (parallel to crowd line)
  NOT toward/away from crowd

Fan Rack Angles

Purpose: Spread burst pattern across sky

Center Tube: 90° vertical (always)
Outer Tubes: Fanned outward

Standard Fan Patterns:

3-Tube Fan:
  - Left: 75° (15° from center)
  - Center: 90°
  - Right: 105° (15° from center)
  - Total spread: 30°

5-Tube Fan:
  - Tubes: 75°, 82.5°, 90°, 97.5°, 105°
  - Total spread: 30°
  - Increment: 7.5°

7-Tube Fan:
  - Tubes: 70°, 77.5°, 85°, 90°, 95°, 102.5°, 110°
  - Total spread: 40°
  - Increment: 6.67°

Maximum Safe Fan Angle: 45° total spread (67.5° to 112.5°)

⚠️ Critical Safety Notes

Shell-Tube Mismatch Dangers

Too Tight (< 0.03” clearance):

  • Shell stuck during loading
  • Pressure cannot build
  • Weak or failed lift
  • Barrel burst risk (tube explosion)

Too Loose (> 0.15” clearance):

  • Pressure leaks around shell
  • Weak lift = low break altitude
  • Shell tumbles during flight
  • Unpredictable trajectory

Tube Too Short:

  • Shell protrudes from tube top
  • EXTREME DANGER: Direct ignition possible
  • Lift charge insufficient space
  • “Flowerpot” risk (ground burst)

Rack Stability Requirements

Minimum Stability Test: "Boot Kick Test"

Kick rack firmly with boot heel
→ Rack should not tip, slide, or shift

If rack moves: Add more anchoring

Safety Distances

Minimum Spectator Distances:

1.75"-2.5" shells: 70 feet (21.3 m)
3"-4" shells: 150 feet (45.7 m)
5"-6" shells: 300 feet (91.4 m)
8"-12" shells: 500+ feet (152+ m)

Fallout Radius (all directions):
  - Add 50% to spectator distance
  - Clear of buildings, vehicles, trees

🎨 3D Modeling Checklist

Tube Model Requirements

  • Correct ID/OD ratio for shell size
  • Wall thickness accurate (2.5-6.4mm range)
  • Base plug thickness appropriate
  • Length matches standard or specified
  • Material texture (HDPE sheen vs fiberglass matte)
  • Color coding if applicable
  • Slight taper optional (manufacturing reality)

Rack Model Requirements

  • Tube spacing: exact center-to-center distances
  • Base plate thickness: 19mm (3/4”) for wood
  • Fastener positions: countersunk, not through tubes
  • Wood grain direction: vertical on supports
  • Feet/anchoring geometry functional
  • Stability ratio: width ≥ 2× height
  • Perpendicular orientation to crowd
  • Fan angles accurate if angled rack

Assembly Validation

  • All tubes sit firmly in holders
  • No tube wobble or loose fit
  • Base plate flat and stable
  • Anchoring points accessible
  • Weight distribution balanced
  • Rack passes “boot kick test” (if physical)
